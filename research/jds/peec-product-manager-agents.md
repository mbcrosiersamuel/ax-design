ABOUT PEEC AI

Peec AI is the analytics platform for AI search. People increasingly discover products and make buying decisions through ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Google's AI Overviews instead of traditional search, and most brands have no idea where they show up in those answers. Peec gives marketing, SEO, and growth teams the data to see exactly that: how visible their brand is across AI platforms, how they compare to competitors, and where to win.

We are defining this category, and the market is moving with us. Peec is a Series A company backed by 20VC and Singular, past $15M ARR, with a 80+ person team across our Berlin HQ and our new New York office.




WHAT YOU'LL DO

 - Own the agentic product surface at Peec AI, from the harness itself to the experience built around it

 - Build agents that act proactively on customer data, alongside a pod of data scientists, AI engineers and a frontend engineer

 - Work directly with our largest agency and brand customers, building specific agents for a handful of them first and scale it further to other users

 - Turn what works for those first customers into product everyone can use

 - Hold a high bar on interface quality

 


WHO YOU ARE

 - You have built an agent harness or a proactive agentic system, something that looks at a user's data or the work in front of them and then does the work rather than just suggesting it

 - Your agent experience is in a B2B or prosumer context

 - 3+ building excellent software products, ideally at a startup or fast-moving company

 - Real working experience alongside data scientists and AI engineers. You do not need to have been one, but you need to speak the language

 - Strong customer-facing instincts. You are comfortable in the room with a large customer, scoping what they actually need

 - High design and UI standards

 - You operate with very little direction and are happy owning an entire surface on your own

 - Excited to be in our Berlin office  five days a week

On the agent requirement: a job, a serious side project, a university project or a hackathon build all count, as long as it shipped and you can walk us through it. Beyond that we care far more about what you have built than how long you have been building.
